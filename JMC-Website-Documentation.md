## 1. What is this website?

The **Jammu Municipal Corporation (JMC)** website is the official government website for the civic body that manages Jammu city — the winter capital of J&K Union Territory.

**What JMC does:** JMC handles sanitation, water supply, road maintenance, property tax, solid waste management, public health, building permissions, street lighting, and all civic services for Jammu city residents.

**What this website does:** It lets citizens access JMC services online — pay fees, file complaints, view notices and tenders, check RTI disclosures, find official contact numbers, and more — without visiting any office.

**Who is it for:** Citizens of Jammu city, contractors, businesses, government officials, and anyone needing information about JMC services.

## 4. Pay Online — Making Payments

**Page:** `/pay-online`  
**What it does:** Lets citizens pay JMC fees and charges online using BillDesk (government-approved payment gateway).

### Payment Categories

#### Option 1: Tender Fee
**For:** Contractors who want to participate in JMC tenders and need to pay the tender document/processing fee.

**Form fields you need to fill:**
- Full Name (required)
- Parentage — S/o, D/o, W/o (required)
- Mobile Number — 10-digit (required)
- Email (optional)
- Address
- NIT / Tender No. — e.g. NIT-2025/001 (required)
- NIT / Tender Date (required)
- Tender Details — brief description
- Amount in Rupees (required)

#### Option 2: Other Fee
**For:** Any other JMC municipal dues — license fees, departmental charges, user charges, etc.

**Form fields you need to fill:**
- Department (required) — select from:
  - Health Section
  - Veterinary Services
  - Transport Section
  - Khilafwarzi Section
  - Revenue Section
  - Miscellaneous
  - Building Permission
- Type of Fee — e.g. "License Renewal" (required)
- Payment Details — brief description (required)
- Zone (required) — select from: Zone A, Zone B, Zone C, Zone D, Zone E
- Full Name (required)
- Mobile Number — 10-digit (required)
- Email (optional)
- Address (required)
- Amount in Rupees (required)

### How payment works (step by step)
1. Go to `/pay-online` on the website
2. Select your payment category (Tender Fee or Other Fee)
3. Fill the form with your details
4. Click "Pay Now"
5. You will be redirected to the **BillDesk** secure payment page
6. Complete payment using your preferred method (card, net banking, UPI, etc.)
7. After payment, you are redirected back to the JMC website
8. You see a **Payment Success** or **Payment Failed** screen
9. If successful, you can **download a PDF receipt** which includes:
   - JMC branding
   - Transaction ID and Order ID
   - Your name and contact details
   - Amount paid and date
   - Fee type and department
   - A QR code linking to the transaction

### If payment fails
- Check your bank statement to confirm no deduction
- If money was deducted but payment shows "Failed", wait 24 hours for auto-reversal
- Contact JMC helpline: **18001807207** (Toll Free)

## 14. About JMC — History, Vision, Mission

**Page:** `/about`

### About
Jammu Municipal Corporation (JMC) is the urban local body responsible for the civic administration of Jammu city. It was established under the Jammu & Kashmir Municipal Corporation Act. JMC provides sanitation, water supply, road maintenance, property tax management, solid waste management, and public health services.

JMC covers all wards of Jammu city and operates under various Government of India schemes including Smart Cities Mission and Swachh Bharat Mission.

### Vision
To make Jammu a clean, green, smart, and liveable city — a model of efficient urban governance that places citizens at the centre of all its activities.

### Mission
To provide efficient, transparent, and accountable civic services to all residents of Jammu city, leveraging technology and best practices in urban management.

### History
The history of municipal governance in Jammu dates back to the Dogra rulers. The Jammu Municipal Committee was one of the earliest civic bodies in the region, later upgraded to the Jammu Municipal Corporation under the J&K Municipal Corporation Act. Over the decades, JMC has expanded its jurisdiction and modernised operations through e-governance and citizen-centric initiatives.

## 23. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

**Q: What is the JMC website for?**
A: It is the official website of Jammu Municipal Corporation. Citizens can pay fees, file complaints, view notices, find officer contacts, check tenders, access RTI documents, and use many e-governance services.

**Q: Is the website free to use?**
A: Yes, the website is completely free. You only pay when making online payments for JMC fees/charges.

**Q: Does the website work on mobile phones?**
A: Yes, the website is fully responsive and works on all devices — phones, tablets, and desktops.

### Payments

**Q: How do I pay tender fee online?**
A: Go to `/pay-online`, select "Tender Fee", fill in your details (name, parentage, NIT/Tender number, date, amount), and click Pay Now. You'll be redirected to BillDesk for secure payment.

**Q: How do I pay other JMC fees?**
A: Go to `/pay-online`, select "Other Fee", choose your department and zone, fill your details and amount, then pay via BillDesk.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: BillDesk supports credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and UPI.

**Q: I paid but didn't get a receipt. What do I do?**
A: If payment was successful, you should see a success screen with a download button. If you missed it, contact JMC at 18001807207 with your transaction details.

**Q: My payment failed but money was deducted. What now?**
